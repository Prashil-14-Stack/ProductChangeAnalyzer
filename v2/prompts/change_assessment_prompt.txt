==========================================================
RULES
==========================================================

The difference_summary must be generated by comparing the
Previous Value and New Value.

The difference_summary should:

• Mention all important numeric changes.
• Mention all added text.
• Mention all removed text.
• Mention changed wording where meaningful.
• Ignore formatting-only differences.
• Be concise but complete.

The Business Impact must explain WHY the changes matter,
not merely repeat the differences.

Do not invent impacts that are unsupported.

If the changes are purely editorial,
state that no business behaviour is affected.

Return ONLY valid JSON.

Do not include markdown.

Do not include explanations.

==========================================================
INPUT
==========================================================

Parameter Name

{{PARAMETER_NAME}}

Status

{{STATUS}}

Previous Value

{{OLD_VALUE}}

New Value

{{NEW_VALUE}}

Difference Summary

{{DIFFERENCE_SUMMARY}}

==========================================================
TASK
==========================================================

Perform TWO activities.

----------------------------------------
STEP 1
----------------------------------------

Identify ALL meaningful business differences between the
Previous Value and the New Value.

Include:

• Numeric changes
• Added text
• Removed text
• Modified wording
• Changed eligibility
• Changed benefits
• Changed limits
• Changed policy terms
• Changed premium values
• Changed maturity values
• Changed dates
• Changed conditions

Summarize the differences in business language.

Examples:

- Income Period changed from 99 years to 120 years.
- Maximum Policy Term increased from 20 years to 40 years.
- Maximum Sum Assured increased from Rs.25 lakh to Rs.50 lakh.
- Text "Prashil Wanjari Business Analyst" was added.
- Premium wording changed from "single/limited/regular" to "single/regular".

Do NOT omit textual additions.

----------------------------------------
STEP 2
----------------------------------------

Based on those differences,
analyse ONLY the business implications.

Determine:

1. Business Impact
2. Risk
3. Priority
4. Business Criticality
5. Affected Teams
6. Testing Recommendation
7. Remarks
8. Executive Summary

==========================================================
RULES
==========================================================

Do NOT describe what changed.

The Difference Summary already explains that.

Instead explain WHY the change matters.

Do NOT invent business impacts that are unsupported.

If the change is only editorial or documentation related,
clearly state that no product behaviour is affected.

If the change affects eligibility,
benefits,
premium,
maturity,
death benefit,
surrender,
loan,
taxation,
riders,
policy servicing,
claims,
or compliance,
explain the implications.

Return ONLY valid JSON.

Do not include markdown.

Do not include explanations.
